    Transcription

    Only affects calls picked up from now on -- turning this on never transcribes existing recordings. Anyone can still transcribe an individual past call on demand from the dashboard.

    Historical backfill & export

    Run historical backfill

    Walks the account's entire call history and captures anything not already here. Useful after a gap, or before turning on GHL's auto-delete for call recordings -- run this first so nothing is lost. Can take anywhere from under a minute to over an hour depending on call volume; runs in the background, so it's safe to navigate away.

    Export

    Download every stored recording as one ZIP file, organized by contact. Can take a while for a large amount of history -- the download starts as soon as it's ready and streams as it goes.
