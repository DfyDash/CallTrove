    Analytics

    Usage and estimated cost across every active account, combined. Estimated transcribe cost is exactly that, an estimate (transcribed minutes × AWS's per-minute rate), not a real AWS bill -- the shared server/database cost isn't included since it doesn't grow per client. For one account's own numbers, click into it from the Accounts tab.

    Cost & revenue

    Real recorded cost and revenue across every active account, combined -- not a live estimate, each figure comes from a permanent ledger entry written at the moment it happened, at whatever rate was actually in effect then (see the Transcription billing rates in each account's own Settings). Changing a rate in the future never rewrites what a past month actually earned. Storage has no revenue line -- no client-facing storage rate has ever been agreed, so it's tracked purely as AWS cost CallTrove itself absorbs, never billed through. For one account's own numbers, click into it from the Accounts tab.

    Activity

    Every account cancel/restore/delete across every tenant -- whether triggered by an operator here or by the account owner themselves from their own Settings. Each tenant's own Activity log (in their Settings) never shows these -- this is the only place they're all visible together.
