Security & privacy

CallTrove is built for teams that handle sensitive health information over the phone, hosted on infrastructure that meets strict healthcare privacy requirements (HIPAA). Every protection listed below is already live in the product today — none of it is "coming soon."

Encrypted end to end

Your recordings, transcripts, and account information are locked down with encryption both while stored and while being sent — no shortcuts, no exceptions.

Every access is logged

We keep a permanent record of every time someone views or downloads a call — who did it, when, and whether they were allowed to.

Accounts stay isolated

Each business's data is kept completely separate from every other business on CallTrove. Team members only ever see the calls they're supposed to, and an administrator has to specifically approve any broader access.

Extra login protection

Every account can turn on an extra security step at sign-in, using a free authenticator app on your phone — at no extra cost. We recommend it for anyone who can access call recordings.

How we use email

We only email people who've created a CallTrove account, and only for account security: sign-in verification codes and account notices tied to that account. We never send marketing email, and we never purchase, import, or email a list. Bounces and spam complaints are processed automatically and permanently suppress future sends to that address.
