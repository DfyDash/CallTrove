Security & privacy

CallTrove is built for teams that handle protected health information over the phone, on HIPAA-eligible AWS infrastructure. Every safeguard below is live in the product today, not a roadmap item.

Encrypted end to end

Recordings, transcripts, and account data are encrypted at rest and in transit, with no exceptions carved out for convenience.

Every access is logged

A permanent, append-only audit log records who viewed or downloaded a call, when, and whether the request was denied.

Agencies stay isolated

Each agency's data is walled off from every other agency's. A rep only ever sees the calls they're scoped to; cross-account access needs an explicit admin grant.

Two-factor authentication

Every account can turn on authenticator-app two-factor sign-in at no extra cost. We recommend it for anyone with access to recordings.

How we use email

We only email people who've created a CallTrove account, and only for account security: sign-in verification codes and account notices tied to that account. We never send marketing email, and we never purchase, import, or email a list. Bounces and spam complaints are processed automatically and permanently suppress future sends to that address.
